----------------The Evolution of AI in the Educational Sector---------------------

The integration  of Artificial Intelligence (AI) into education marks one of the most
significant shifts in pedagogical history. For decades, the  classroom model remained
largely unchanged, relying on a one-size-fits-all approach. However, the emergence of 
Large Language Models and machine learning is fundamentally altering how knowledge is
produced, shared, and consumed. In 2026, AI is no longer just a futuristic concept
but a daily assistant for both students and educators. It acts as a bridge between
traditional textbooks and the vast, dynamic world of digital information. The primary
goal of this transformation is to enhance human potential, providing tools that can
handle repetitive tasks while leaving room for critical thinking and creativity.
