---------------The Path Forward: Predictions for 2030------------------

As we look toward the end of the decade, the synergy between human 
intelligence and artificial intelligence will likely define the "New Normal"
in schools and universities. We expect to see more immersive environments,
where AI-powered Virtual Reality (VR) allows students to experience history
or science in three dimensions. The future of education is not about replacing
the teacher with a machine. Instead, it is about the evolution of the teacher 
into a "learning architect." While AI provides the data and the tools, the human
element remains essential for teaching ethics, empathy, and social collaboration.
To succeed, we must embrace these technologies with a critical mind and a commitment
to lifelong learning.
